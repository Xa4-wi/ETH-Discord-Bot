Cody Discord Bot

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information Cody may process when you use its Discord features.

Information Cody may process

Cody may process information necessary to operate the bot, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server ID
  • Discord role information
  • Discord interaction and command information
  • Team information if users register teams through Cody

How information is used

Information is used only to provide ETH Battlecode and Cody functionality. Data is not sold, and unnecessary data is not intentionally collected.

Discord

Discord processes information according to its own policies. Your use of Discord remains subject to those policies.

Deletion requests

For privacy or deletion requests, please contact the ETH Battlecode organizers through the official ETH Battlecode Discord server.
